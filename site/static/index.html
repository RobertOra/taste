How good is your music taste?

Every new song or artist you get into is a small bet. This checks how your bets did: did the music you got into go on to take off, compared with similar music people got into at the same time? Ranked against about 15,000 music enthusiasts, using the method from a study of whether good taste is real.

Everything runs in this browser. Your files never leave your computer.

How to get your Spotify data
  1. Open spotify.com → Account → Privacy settings.
  2. Under Download your data, tick Extended streaming history and request it.
  3. Spotify emails a link within a few days (sometimes up to 30). Download the zip and drop it above.

The extended history covers your whole account. The basic "Account data" covers only the last year, which is too recent to score: each find needs a year to see how it did. You need at least two years of history and a hundred or so songs or artists you really got into.

Reference data: ListenBrainz listening (CC0), 2005–2026. Scores compare you with ListenBrainz listeners, who are keen music fans, not with Spotify's whole audience. The study.
