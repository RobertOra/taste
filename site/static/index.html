How to get your Spotify data
  1. Open spotify.com → Account → Privacy settings.
  2. Under Download your data, tick Extended streaming history and request it.
  3. Spotify emails a link within a few days (sometimes up to 30). Download the zip and drop it above.

The extended history covers your whole account. The basic "Account data" covers only the last year, which is too recent to score: each find needs a year to see how it did. You need at least two years of history and a hundred or so songs or artists you really got into (played in 4+ separate weeks, the 4th within 6 months of first hearing).

How to get your YouTube data
  1. Open takeout.google.com, click Deselect all, then tick My Activity.
  2. Click All activity data included, choose only YouTube, and create the export.
  3. Google emails a link, usually within minutes or hours. Download the zip and drop it above.

Use My Activity, not "YouTube and YouTube Music": My Activity has your full history, YouTube Music included. If Google splits the export into several zips or folders, add them all, one after another.

We pick out the music: YouTube Music, "Artist - Topic" channels, and videos whose title or channel matches a known song. Everything else is ignored.

Reference data: ListenBrainz listening (CC0), 2005–2026. Scores compare you with ListenBrainz listeners, who are keen music fans, not with everyone on Spotify or YouTube. The study.
